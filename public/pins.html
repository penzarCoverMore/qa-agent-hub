Review Pins

Drop a pin directly on any saucedemo.com page to leave a note with a name, description, and severity — right where you noticed it.

📌 QA Pin
  1. Drag the 📌 QA Pin button to your browser's bookmarks bar (make sure it's visible — Ctrl+Shift+B in Chrome).
  2. Open any saucedemo.com page.
  3. Click the 📌 QA Pin bookmark. A small "QA Agent Hub" helper tab will open the first time — leave it open in the background, it's what saves your pins.
  4. Click anywhere on the saucedemo page to drop a pin (name, description, severity).
  5. Click the bookmark again on that page any time to see the pins you've already left there.

The button above is deliberately not clickable here — it only works dragged into your bookmarks bar.
